About Dr. Anderson

From Training Athletes
to Reading Labs

Dr. Anderson's path started in 2008, writing and building training programs for athletes and bodybuilders. That work led him to a Master's in Nutrition and Human Performance, and from there to chiropractic school at Logan University.

At Logan he found Systems Health Care, an Applied Kinesiology approach to finding the dysfunction that standard exams miss. He was a senior intern at the Viscerosomatic Center in Chesterfield, Missouri, where he earned the center's Achievement Award for his clinical work.

He practiced in Charleston, West Virginia, before bringing his practice to Elk River in 2021. Today most of his patients come in with thyroid, gut, hormone, or autoimmune problems, often after being told their labs are normal. His goal is to explain what is actually going on, in plain language, and fix the cause instead of chasing symptoms.

In 2026 the International College of Applied Kinesiology gave him the Dr. Wally Schmitt Award, and he spoke at the ICAK International Conference.
