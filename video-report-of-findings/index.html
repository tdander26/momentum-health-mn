Not Local? No Problem.

Video Report of Findings

You don't need to come to Minnesota to get your labs done and reviewed. Get your blood drawn close to home, and I'll walk you through your results on video.

How It Works

Four Simple Steps

1

Get your blood drawn near you

Go to a LabCorp or Quest close to home. No trip to Minnesota needed.

2

Results come to both of us

Your results come back to you and to me, so I see everything at the same time you do.

3

I record your video

I go through your labs, tell you what's going on, and explain what I'd recommend doing about it.

4

Watch it on your own time

It's a recorded video, not a live call, so you can rewatch it as many times as you like.

Good to Know

Before You Decide

How it's billed. The video is billed by time, just like a regular appointment. Most run about 30 minutes.

The one trade-off. Because it's a recorded video, we won't have the back-and-forth we'd have in the office. Some people are totally fine with that. If you'd rather talk it through in person, you're always welcome to come in.

Your Options

Pick What Works for You

There's no wrong choice here.

Most common

Labs + Video Report

Get your labs drawn locally and I'll record a video going over your results. A great starting point if a trip isn't in the cards right now.

In person

Make the Trip

No strings

Just the Labs

If you only want the labs, I'm happy to order those too. There's no obligation to follow up with me.
